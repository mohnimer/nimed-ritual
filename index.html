02 / Magnetic Rituals

Magnetism,
made tangible.

Some RITUAL objects contain permanent magnetic elements. We are leaning into that honestly: as a material feature, a point of curiosity and a reason to build a deliberate daily ritual around the object.

Research note

Interesting. Studied. Not magic.

Static magnets have been studied in areas such as pain perception, circulation and musculoskeletal comfort, but results are mixed and no conclusive therapeutic benefit has been established. RITUAL does not present static magnets as a medical treatment.

Magnetic bracelet edit

Only models where the magnetic element is part of the known construction are included here. Tap a model to open the existing wholesale product detail.

Magnet safety. Loose high-powered magnets must be kept away from children and must never be swallowed. Magnetic products should also be kept away from implanted pacemakers and ICDs; major cardiac-device guidance recommends at least 15 cm / 6 in separation. This range is presented as a lifestyle collection, not as a medical treatment.
